Decision Ledger · Personal project · 4 Sep 2026

Before trusting the test, check that the defect was inserted

A mutation test can look healthy even when it never changed the code. The first run stopped because its target appeared twice. Requiring a unique target made that false pass visible before any result could be trusted.

What happened & what remains

The repair used a temporary copy of the checker, unique executable anchors and an unchanged control. The dated run caught all 10 declared mutations while the control survived. That is the scope of that run, not a claim that every possible defect is detectable.

Render-for-X · Personal experiment · 16–17 Sep 2026

The score improved. The comparison did not hold up.

An early comparison suggested that a tailored brief worked better, but the same agent wrote and scored both versions with the answers in context. That improvement claim was withdrawn. A later blind evaluation still did not establish a reader benefit.

What happened & what remains

In the later experiment, both versions passed the fidelity check. Reader feedback did not establish a benefit from tailoring; reading order and language also differed. The useful next question was about making the overview easier to enter, rather than claiming a better personalized system.

先確認錯誤真的被放進去，再相信測試

Render-for-X · 個人實驗 · 2026-09-16–17

分數變好了，但這個比較站不住腳

早期比較看似顯示客製摘要比較好，卻是同一個 agent 在已知答案的上下文中寫稿、自評。這個提升宣稱後來被撤回；後續盲評也仍不足以證明讀者真的受益。

經過與待補細節

後續實驗中，兩份版本都通過內容保真檢查，但讀者回饋沒有證明客製化的好處；閱讀順序與語言也不同。下一步因此轉向讓概要更容易讀進去，而不是宣稱個人化系統已經比較好。
