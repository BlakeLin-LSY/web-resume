Study Atlas

One study, several ways to read it.

I wanted research outputs that fit a reader's time without losing the question, the reasoning, or the sources. Study Atlas makes the reading route part of the delivery.

Two cards from the September 2026 VGGT study: a reported experiment and an open question. The original bilingual text is preserved here in a compact layout. Open a card to read the reasoning.

Paper evidence · §4.3, Table 3

At inference time point maps derived from depth and cameras are more accurate than the direct point-map head

Keep multiple supervised paths during training then use known relations to choose the more reliable delivery path

Read the card

Table 3 compares two point-cloud routes from the same model on ETH3D. The direct point-map head has Overall error 0.709, while unprojecting depth with predicted cameras yields 0.677. The authors attribute the gain to decomposing point-map estimation into simpler depth and camera subproblems. Multi-task training therefore does not require every direct head to be the final delivery path.

Open question · design hypothesis

The paper does not establish whether its design principles work for everyday cross-domain tasks

Use shared context, redundant supervision, and optional refinement as an experiment checklist, not a validated recipe

Read the card

Training spans many real and synthetic 3D datasets, while experiments cover camera pose, multi-view depth, point maps, matching, novel-view synthesis, and tracking. Broad 3D-vision evidence still excludes daily domains such as software engineering, incident diagnosis, or business decisions. The five-step framework in this study is therefore a design hypothesis inferred from the paper's architecture, ablations, and optimization results. Each transfer should predefine its baseline, cost, quality metric, and fallback condition.

The reader problem

A one-minute briefing and a thirty-minute explanation need different routes through the same material. A shorter version still needs a conclusion and enough context to stand on its own; a longer version needs room for the derivation.

Study Atlas organizes a supported paper, repository, web source, or topic into a study with bilingual knowledge cards, source locators, and explicit narrative order. Each reading budget has its own route through those cards.

  1. 01Source & question
  2. 02Authored Semantic IR
  3. 03Compiler & checks
  4. 04Bilingual reader views

The choice I made

I made the authored meaning explicit in a typed Semantic IR: the question, claims, sources, bilingual card content, and reading order. A Python compiler expands that into study files, and a separate renderer builds the reader HTML.

This keeps presentation changes separate from the knowledge being presented. It requires more authoring structure, but gives the compiler something concrete to check and regenerate. The normal new-study path also refuses to overwrite an existing destination.

What it produces today

Existing outputs include English and Traditional Chinese files for T1, T5 and T30 reading depths, plus HTML readers with language and depth controls. The excerpt above shows two cards from an actual delivery.

The historical walkthrough below follows a September 13, 2026 reading-budget rejection and revision, with the evidence for each step. Passing a delivery check does not establish scientific correctness or faster human reading; those require source review and rehearsal.

讀者的問題

一分鐘的簡報和三十分鐘的說明，需要不同的閱讀路徑。短版本仍要有能獨立成立的結論與脈絡，長版本則需要空間展開推導。

Study Atlas 把論文、程式碼或網頁資料整理成雙語知識卡，保存來源位置與敘事順序，再為不同閱讀深度安排卡片路徑。

  1. 01來源與問題
  2. 02結構化研究內容（Semantic IR）
  3. 03編譯與檢查
  4. 04雙語閱讀版本

我的關鍵選擇

我用具型別的中介表示（Semantic IR）保存問題、主張、來源、雙語內容與閱讀順序。Python 編譯器把它展開成研究檔案，再由獨立的 renderer 產生 HTML。

內容與呈現分開，讓版面可以修改而不必重寫研究內容。代價是作者需要填寫更明確的結構，但編譯器也因此能檢查並重新產生交付檔；建立新研究時會拒絕覆寫既有目錄。

目前可看見的結果

既有產出包含 T1、T5、T30 三種閱讀深度的雙語稿件，以及可切換語言與深度的 HTML reader。上方摘錄展示其中兩張實際交付的卡片。
