Personal project · Engineering case study

Study Atlas

One study, several ways to read it.

I wanted research outputs that fit a reader's time without losing the question, the reasoning, or the sources. Study Atlas makes the reading route part of the delivery.

  • Python
  • Semantic IR
  • Knowledge graphs

The reader problem

A one-minute briefing and a thirty-minute explanation need different routes through the same material. A shorter version still needs a conclusion and enough context to stand on its own; a longer version needs room for the derivation.

Study Atlas organizes a supported paper, repository, web source, or topic into a study with bilingual knowledge cards, source locators, and explicit narrative order. Each reading budget has its own route through those cards.

The choice I made

I made the authored meaning explicit in a typed Semantic IR: the question, claims, sources, bilingual card content, and reading order. A Python compiler expands that into study files, and a separate renderer builds the reader HTML.

This keeps presentation changes separate from the knowledge being presented. It requires more authoring structure, but gives the compiler something concrete to check and regenerate. The normal new-study path also refuses to overwrite an existing destination.

What it produces today

Existing study outputs include English and Traditional Chinese reading files for T1, T5, and T30, plus HTML reader pages with language and depth controls. The Semantic IR product tour provides a concrete local artifact showing the path from authored input to reader output.

A personal live paper request on September 13, 2026 made the budget constraint concrete. Its delivery record describes a 6.0-minute T5 TALK plan above the 5.0-minute limit, then a compact-card revision. The final receipt records a passing 4.6-minute plan on the historical Contract 1.8 path.

Those minutes are calculated TALK card budgets, not timed reading or elapsed processing time. Compiled outputs establish a reading delivery; whether the claims are sound and a reader understands them still requires source review and human rehearsal.

讀者的問題

一分鐘的 briefing 和三十分鐘的說明，需要不同的敘事路徑。短版本仍需要能獨立成立的結論與脈絡，長版本則需要空間展開推導。

我的關鍵選擇

我用 typed Semantic IR 明確保存 authored meaning：問題、主張、來源、雙語卡片內容與閱讀順序。Python compiler 把它展開成 study files，另一個 renderer 產生 reader HTML。

這讓 presentation 的修改與被呈現的知識分開。Authoring 需要更多結構，但 compiler 也有具體內容可檢查並重新產生；一般 new-study 路徑另會拒絕覆寫既有 destination。
